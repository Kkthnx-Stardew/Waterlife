# Changelog

## 1.0.0-beta.1

First public build, for testing. 1.0.0 follows once it has been played through.

- Shows the fish, junk and treasure the game would give for a cast at each water tile.
- Fish stay in the water. Squid, eels and bottom dwellers no longer walk off shore or off the map edge.
- The Angler sits in the river north of town, where the game offers it.
- Legendary fish and fixed treasure show only while the game would still offer them, and they update
  when you catch one.
- Settings saved in Generic Mod Config Menu apply at once.
- Another player's warp no longer rebuilds your water in multiplayer.
- Fish from other mods are shown. Items that do not exist are skipped, and water with no fish in it
  shows only a few pieces of junk.
- Stardew Valley Expanded's wall basket shows only when that mod is installed, and follows the
  treasure setting.
- Contains no files from other mods. Fish use the game's own sprites, plus a rare golden fish in
  Sunberry Village and a statue in Stardew Valley Expanded's woods that spawns a floating basket.
